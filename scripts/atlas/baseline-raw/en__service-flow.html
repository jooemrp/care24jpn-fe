How it works

From registration to completion, in four simple steps.

  1. 1

    Registration

    Please enter your information to register as a member in order to use the service.

  2. 2

    Confirmation of your reservation

    Once a care supporter has been matched with you, your reservation will be confirmed and you will be notified via email, LINE, etc.

  3. 3

    Service Start

    A care supporter will visit your home at the scheduled date and time.

  4. 4

    Completion Report

    After the service is completed, the care supporter will send you a report, and the service will be finished.
