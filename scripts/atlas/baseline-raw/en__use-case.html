Use cases

Care 24 Japan adapts to the moments that matter most.

After hospital discharge

Nursing and daily-living support during recovery at home.

Leaving hospital can feel daunting. Care 24 Japan deploys a coordinated team of nurses and certified caregivers from day one — covering medication management, wound care, meals, bathing, and mobility support. We work closely with your family and discharging hospital to ensure a seamless, safe transition back to the comfort of home.

  • Available from the day of discharge
  • Coordinated nurse and caregiver team
  • Medication and wound-care management
  • Rehabilitation and mobility support
  • Regular updates to family members

Dementia care

Gentle supervision and companionship for peace of mind.

Our specially trained caregivers provide compassionate, person-centred dementia care at home. We focus on familiar routines, gentle reminders, reminiscence activities, and safe supervision — helping maintain dignity and reduce anxiety. We also keep family members closely informed, so everyone feels supported.

  • Dementia-trained specialist caregivers
  • 24-hour supervision plans available
  • Reminiscence and communication support
  • Medication and meal management
  • Family updates and counselling support

Respite for families

Temporary cover so family caregivers can rest.

Family caregivers give everything — and they deserve to rest. Care 24 Japan steps in for a few hours or several days, providing the same high-quality care your loved one is used to. Whether you need a short break, a medical appointment, or a holiday, we ensure continuity of care so you can recharge with complete peace of mind.

  • Flexible from a few hours to several days
  • Seamless continuity of existing care routines
  • Rapid response in case of emergency
  • Shared care records for smooth handover
  • Available as regular or spot bookings

End-of-life home care

Compassionate support to spend final days at home.

When the time comes, most people wish to be at home — surrounded by those they love. Care 24 Japan provides dignified, gentle end-of-life care that prioritises comfort and respects the wishes of the individual and their family. We coordinate closely with palliative care teams and GPs to ensure pain is managed and every moment is as peaceful as possible.

  • Coordination with palliative care teams
  • Pain management and physical comfort care
  • Emotional support for client and family
  • Round-the-clock presence and response
  • Space for family to be present and grieve
