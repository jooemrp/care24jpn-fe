<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-heading">After hospital discharge</h2>
<p class="mt-2 text-sm font-medium text-muted">Nursing and daily-living support during recovery at home.</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-base leading-relaxed text-body">Leaving hospital can feel daunting. Care 24 Japan deploys a coordinated team of nurses and certified caregivers from day one — covering medication management, wound care, meals, bathing, and mobility support. We work closely with your family and discharging hospital to ensure a seamless, safe transition back to the comfort of home.</p>
<ul class="mt-6 flex flex-col gap-2">
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Available from the day of discharge</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Coordinated nurse and caregiver team</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Medication and wound-care management</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Rehabilitation and mobility support</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Regular updates to family members</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-heading">Dementia care</h2>
<p class="mt-2 text-sm font-medium text-muted">Gentle supervision and companionship for peace of mind.</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-base leading-relaxed text-body">Our specially trained caregivers provide compassionate, person-centred dementia care at home. We focus on familiar routines, gentle reminders, reminiscence activities, and safe supervision — helping maintain dignity and reduce anxiety. We also keep family members closely informed, so everyone feels supported.</p>
<ul class="mt-6 flex flex-col gap-2">
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Dementia-trained specialist caregivers</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>24-hour supervision plans available</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Reminiscence and communication support</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Medication and meal management</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">

</span>Family updates and counselling support</li>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-heading">Respite for families</h2>
<p class="mt-2 text-sm font-medium text-muted">Temporary cover so family caregivers can rest.</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-base leading-relaxed text-body">Family caregivers give everything — and they deserve to rest. Care 24 Japan steps in for a few hours or several days, providing the same high-quality care your loved one is used to. Whether you need a short break, a medical appointment, or a holiday, we ensure continuity of care so you can recharge with complete peace of mind.</p>
<ul class="mt-6 flex flex-col gap-2">
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">

<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Seamless continuity of existing care routines</li>
<li class="flex items-start gap-2 text-sm text-body">
<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">
</span>Rapid response in case of emergency</li>

<span class="mt-1.5 h-1 w-1 shrink-0 rounded-full bg-primary" aria-hidden="true">

<h2 class="text-2xl font-bold text-heading">End-of-life home care</h2>
<p class="mt-2 text-sm font-medium text-muted">Compassionate support to spend final days at home.</p>
<p class="mt-4 text-base leading-relaxed text-body">When the time comes, most people wish to be at home — surrounded by those they love. Care 24 Japan provides dignified, gentle end-of-life care that prioritises comfort and respects the wishes of the individual and their family. We coordinate closely with palliative care teams and GPs to ensure pain is managed and every moment is as peaceful as possible.</p>
